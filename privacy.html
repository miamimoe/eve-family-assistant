Eve Family Assistant

Privacy and Google data use

Eve is a private household assistant controlled by its account owner. Its Google integration is intended for authorized household use.

Data accessed and used

With the account owner's permission, Eve can access Gmail messages and metadata, calendars and events, Drive files, and content in Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides. Access is limited by the permissions granted to the connected Google account. These services support requested tasks such as finding information, preparing messages, organizing events, and creating or editing files.

Processing and disclosure

Relevant task content may be sent to the configured AI model provider to answer requests and perform tasks. Results, which may include Google data, may be delivered through the household's authorized third-party messaging channels, such as WhatsApp, and are subject to that service's handling. Google data is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally supplied for general-purpose model training by this integration. Eve’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and control

OAuth credentials are stored in a private profile on the owner's computer. Task content may remain in local conversation records, operational logs, generated files, and the connected services, according to their respective retention settings. The owner controls local records and can remove them or request removal from relevant service providers.

The owner can revoke access through their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revocation stops future authorized API access; it does not automatically delete existing files, messages, or local records.

Questions

This integration is operated privately by its account owner. Authorized users should contact the owner through their existing private communication channel. The support address is displayed on Google's authorization screen.
